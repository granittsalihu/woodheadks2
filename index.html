Woodhead-KS has been shaping spaces with exceptional woodworking since 2008. We specialise in bespoke carpentry — from heirloom-quality furniture to full interior transformations — using only the finest sustainably sourced hardwoods: American walnut, white oak, and European ash.

Every piece we build is a collaboration between skilled hands and discerning vision. No mass production, no shortcuts. Just meticulous craftsmanship that stands the test of time.
